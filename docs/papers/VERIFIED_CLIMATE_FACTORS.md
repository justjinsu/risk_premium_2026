## 1. WILDFIRE CLIMATE FACTORS

### Source: World Weather Attribution (2025)
**Study:** "Climate change made weather conditions leading to deadly South Korean wildfires about twice as likely"
**URL:** https://www.worldweatherattribution.org/climate-change-made-weather-conditions-leading-to-deadly-south-korean-wildfires-about-twice-as-likely/

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Current climate attribution | **2.0x** more likely | Fire weather conditions doubled vs pre-industrial |
| Intensity increase | **25%** | Current climate vs pre-industrial |
| Future projection (2100) | **2.0x** additional | Further doubling at 2.6C warming |
| Combined 2100 | **~4.0x** | 2x current × 2x future = ~4x pre-industrial |

### Source: Sun et al. (2023) Earth System Science Data
**DOI:** 10.5194/essd-15-2153-2023
**Title:** Fire weather index data under historical and SSP projections (1850-2100)

| Metric | Value | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| At 3°C warming | **+66%** duration/frequency | Global average fire weather |
| 1-in-10-year events | **3x** duration | Tripling of extreme fire weather duration |
